What to Include

Mention trailhead name, approximate distance from trailhead, and whether the obstacle is passable on foot.

Embeddable HTML & Setup Guide

Copy and paste onto your Twin Falls Trails website

1. Zero-Backend Link (Opens User's Email App)

Works on any static webpage with zero setup. Launches their email app with Falls Trail as the subject.

<a href="mailto:twinfallstrails@gmail.com?subject=Falls%20Trail&body=Any%20reports%20of%20problems%20or%20trail%20conditions">
  Report Falls Trail Conditions
</a>

2. Direct Send Form (No Email App Required)

How to activate direct delivery to twinfallstrails@gmail.com:

  1. Go to web3forms.com (or formspree.io) — both have free tiers with no credit card needed.
  2. Enter twinfallstrails@gmail.com to receive your free Access Key.
  3. Paste that Access Key into the hidden input below, and hikers can submit straight from your page!
<form action="https://api.web3forms.com/submit" method="POST">
  <!-- Replace with your free key from web3forms.com -->
  <input type="hidden" name="access_key" value="YOUR_WEB3FORMS_ACCESS_KEY">
  <input type="hidden" name="subject" value="Falls Trail">

  <label for="report">Trail Condition Report:</label><br>
  <textarea id="report" name="message" rows="4" required>Any reports of problems or trail conditions</textarea><br>

  <button type="submit">Send Trail Report</button>
</form>
