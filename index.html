Report Falls Trail Conditions

Spotted a downed tree, muddy washout, pile of trash or wildlife along Falls Trail? Send a quick report directly to our Trail Coordinator below at twinfallstrails@gmail.com.



Tip: Include approximate mile marker or landmark if known. 43 chars

What to Include

Mention trailhead name, approximate distance from trailhead, and whether the obstacle is passable on foot.

Emergency Notice

For medical emergencies, lost hikers, or active wildfires, always dial 911 immediately instead of using this form.
